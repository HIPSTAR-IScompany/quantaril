# Quantaril Cloud and AQC

Quantaril Cloud is a higher-level concept involving information ontology and distributed observation, not a particular SaaS product, API, RAG system, database, or MCP connector. AQC is one subordinate implementation; the two are not identical.

The source distinguishes a church as an institution from God to explain implementation and the larger vision. Preserve that meaning rather than reducing it to a service catalog.

## Design questions

- Where do state, keys, and authority reside, and what is exchanged?
- What survives a node failure and can be executed again?
- Which observer and evaluation are bound to which revision and World?
- How do meaning and authorship survive a change of representation?

P2P, E2EE, sovereignty, portable thought logs, and distributed observation are design directions. Names do not establish their achievement: retain implementations, measurements, and unresolved conditions separately.
